Mooze Releases

Unless you own contemporary hardware, you must use an emulator for 1994-1999 vintage MS-DOS PC, such as DOSBox, to run our releases. Our music modules (MOD, XM) can be played with modern media player software.

Our releases predate modern copyright licenses. We have retroactively licensed all our releases under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, except when we state another Creative Commons license within documentation of a specific release.
